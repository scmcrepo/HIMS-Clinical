================================================================================
       NHCX INSURANCE & CLAIMS STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP) & USER MANUAL
       End-to-End Operational Guide for Billing, Insurance & Finance Staff
================================================================================

1. INTRODUCTION & USER ROLES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) enables any billing executive, insurance desk
staff, accountant, or hospital administrator to perform Broadcast Policy Lookup,
Cashless Pre-Authorisation, and Final Claims Settlement seamlessly without any prior
training or external assistance.

USER ROLES & REQUIRED PERMISSIONS:
  - Reception & Billing Desk : Broadcast Policy Search, Policy Linking, Coverage Printing
  - Insurance Executive      : Cashless Pre-Auth Submissions, Pre-Auth Enhancements, Query Answers
  - Accounts Manager         : Final Claims Submission, Payment Tracking, TPA Reconciliation


================================================================================
2. WORKFLOW 1: BROADCAST INSURANCE POLICY LOOKUP (FIND POLICIES)
================================================================================

📍 WHERE TO ACCESS ON PORTAL:
  1. Login to HIMS Portal.
  2. Click "Registration / Patients" in the left sidebar menu.
  3. Search for the patient and click to open their profile.
  4. Select the "Insurance" tab on the profile page.
  5. Scroll to the "Find Policies" panel.
  6. URL: http://localhost:5173/patients/{patientId}?tab=insurance

📤 STEP-BY-STEP OPERATIONAL INSTRUCTIONS:
  Step 1: On the "Find Policies" panel, enter the Patient's ABHA Address (e.g., ravi@abdm)
          OR 10-digit mobile number.
  Step 2: Click the "Send OTP" button. The patient receives an authorization SMS OTP.
  Step 3: Ask the patient for the 6-digit OTP, enter it into the text box, and click "Confirm OTP".
          (Note: In testing/simulation mode, enter any 6-digit number like 123456 or 000000).
  Step 4: The system broadcasts a policy search across ALL insurance companies and TPAs in India via NHCX.

👁️ EXPECTED PORTAL FEEDBACK & RESULTS:
  - The "Discovered Policies" list populates automatically on screen displaying:
    * Insurance Company Name & TPA Name
    * Policy Number & Policy Type (Individual, Family Floater, PM-JAY, Corporate Group)
    * Policy Status Badge: ACTIVE (Green) / LAPSED (Red) / EXHAUSTED (Amber)
    * Total Sum Insured & Currently Available Sum Insured Balance
    * Room Rent Daily Limits (e.g., Rs. 5,000/day or 1% Sum Insured)
    * Co-Payment % (e.g., 10% co-pay payable by patient)
    * Exclusions & Pre-Existing Disease (PED) Waiting Period Status
  Step 5: Click the "Link to Patient" button beside the policy. It is now attached to the
          patient's profile for automated billing and pre-auth calculations.

💡 TROUBLESHOOTING & EDGE CASES:
  - Issue: "That OTP was not accepted" error message appears.
    Solution: Verify OTP with patient or click "Send OTP" to generate a fresh one.
  - Issue: No policies discovered.
    Solution: Check if patient has a different mobile number linked to their policy card.

3. WORKFLOW 2: COVERAGE & BENEFITS BREAKDOWN
================================================================================

📍 WHERE TO ACCESS ON PORTAL:
  1. Open Patient Profile page (/patients/{patientId}).
  2. Click the "Insurance" tab.
  3. Look at the top "Coverage & Benefits" panel.

📤 STEP-BY-STEP OPERATIONAL INSTRUCTIONS:
  - Auto-loaded once policy is linked.
  - Review live sum insured balance, room rent limits, and co-payment obligations.
  - Click the "Print for patient" button to generate a printable summary sheet for the patient.


================================================================================
4. WORKFLOW 3: CASHLESS PRE-AUTHORISATION REQUEST (IP ADMISSIONS)
================================================================================

📍 WHERE TO ACCESS ON PORTAL:
  1. Click "Insurance" in the left sidebar menu.
  2. Select "Pre-authorisations" sub-menu item.
  3. URL: http://localhost:5173/insurance/preauth

📤 STEP-BY-STEP OPERATIONAL INSTRUCTIONS:
  Step 1: Click the "+ New Pre-Auth Request" button on top right.
  Step 2: Select the Patient's active IP Admission encounter from the dropdown.
  Step 3: Build treatment cost estimate line items:
          - Daily Room & Nursing Charges
          - Surgery & Anaesthesia Fees
          - Expected Pharmacy & Consumables
          - Diagnostic Investigations & Implants
  Step 4: Review total estimate and click "Submit Pre-Auth to NHCX".

👁️ EXPECTED PORTAL FEEDBACK & RESULTS:
  - The request lands in the "Pre-Authorisation Tracker Table" with real-time status badges:
    * SUBMITTED (Blue)    : Sent to insurer TPA, awaiting medical review.
    * APPROVED (Green)    : Approved! Displays Sanctioned Cashless Amount (e.g., Rs. 75,000)
                            and Approved Room Category.
    * QUERY_RAISED (Amber): Insurer requested extra documents. Click row to read query note
                            and upload requested lab/radiology reports directly.
    * REJECTED (Red)      : Claim denied. Displays explicit rejection reason from insurer.

💡 PRE-AUTH ENHANCEMENT (WHEN STAY EXTENDS):
  If treatment cost increases during stay:
  Step 1: Click on the active Pre-Auth row in /insurance/preauth.
  Step 2: Click "Submit Enhancement Request".
  Step 3: Enter revised total estimate and clinical justification.
  Step 4: Upon approval, the approved sum updates automatically on screen.


================================================================================
5. WORKFLOW 4: CLAIMS & PAYMENTS CONTROL TOWER
================================================================================

📍 WHERE TO ACCESS ON PORTAL:
  1. Click "Insurance" in left sidebar -> Select "Claims & Payments".
  2. URL: http://localhost:5173/insurance/claims

📤 STEP-BY-STEP OPERATIONAL INSTRUCTIONS:
  Step 1: Select the discharged patient's final bill from the queue.
  Step 2: Verify final bill total, discharge summary notes, and pre-auth approved limit.
  Step 3: Click "Submit Claim to NHCX".

👁️ EXPECTED PORTAL FEEDBACK & RESULTS:
  - Claim status updates to "CLAIM_SUBMITTED".
  - Dashboard tracks TPA payment settlement receipts, insurer deductions, and patient co-pay.
